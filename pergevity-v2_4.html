Two services, one method

What your customers say. What an independent audit finds. Together, the full picture.

Study

Member & Guest Study

A structured survey of your members, guests or hotel clientele — who they are, how they use your facility, what they miss, and how satisfied they really are.

  • Standardised online questionnaire, 5 thematic blocks — demographics, usage, wishes, satisfaction, retention
  • Questions within each block tailored to your facility type — golf club, hotel gym or sport-tourism operator — not a one-size-fits-all form
  • Optional in-depth interviews for qualitative insight
  • Guest/non-member segment on request — including conversion analysis: what would turn a regular guest into a member?
  • Report with satisfaction index, segment profiles and prioritised recommendations
Field phase 3–4 weeks · anonymous · GDPR-compliant
Audit

Facility Audit

A structured on-site assessment of your sports facility — gym, golf operation, hotel fitness area — against a fixed criteria grid across six categories.

  • Safety & maintenance · staff & expertise · program & offering
  • Equipment & facility · member/guest experience · operations & utilization
  • Each criterion scored on a declared 0–5 scale or measured directly against thresholds, photo-documented, aggregated to category and overall scores
  • Report with traffic-light rating per category, quick wins and mid-term recommendations
Half-day or full-day on site · re-audit option after 6–12 months

Strongest together: the combined audit

The real value sits in the gap between the two: issues the audit finds before members complain about them — and dissatisfaction the audit can't confirm on site, which points to a communication problem rather than a facility problem. One engagement, both perspectives, one report.

The instruments

No black box. This is what we actually measure.

Both instruments run on a fixed, weighted scoring structure — the same blocks and categories every time, so results stay comparable across facilities and across years. The specific questions within that structure are adapted to your facility type: a golf club and a hotel gym aren't asked the same things.

Member & Guest Study

5 question blocks · online survey + optional interviews
  1. Demographics & profileWho your members and guests actually are — age, origin, household, occupation
  2. Membership & usageTenure, frequency, preferred times, how they found you
  3. Behaviour on siteWhich areas get used, which don't — and where investment is misplaced
  4. Wishes & preferencesWhat's missing, what should change — the block clients quote most
  5. Satisfaction & retentionScored satisfaction index, NPS, churn risk — the part that becomes your 0–100 score

Facility Audit

6 categories · 56 criteria · 0–5 judgements + measured metrics
  1. Safety & Maintenance 20%First-aid coverage, emergency readiness, maintenance logs, hazard control, insurance documentation
  2. Staff & Expertise 25%Licenses, experience, continuing education and trainer ratio — split 50/50 into qualification and practice
  3. Program & Offering 15%Training progression, individualization, group sizes, progress tracking
  4. Equipment & Facility 22%Cardio, machines, free weights, functional and mobility zones — plus hygiene, capacity and technology
  5. Member/Guest Experience 10%Response times, onboarding, complaint handling, satisfaction data
  6. Operations & Utilization 8%Utilization data, no-shows, churn, cost transparency, year-over-year benchmarks

How it works

From first call to final report in four steps.

STEP 01

Scoping call

We define what you want to know, which segments matter, and which instrument fits — study, audit, or both.

STEP 02

Field phase

Survey rollout to your members and guests, and/or the structured on-site audit. Minimal effort on your side — the work is ours.

STEP 03

Report & score

Weighted scores, traffic-light bands, segment profiles and a prioritised list: quick wins first, investments second.

STEP 04

Presentation & follow-up

Results presented to your management, on site or remote. Optional re-run after 6–12 months to measure progress — and, where it fits, an ongoing performance partnership.

Pergevity is a boutique sport-science consultancy based in Mauritius. Every engagement is led personally by its principal, Oliver Zänkert — a sport scientist with more than 25 years in the field as coach, operator and consultant. That's deliberate: you get the senior expert on site, not a junior team working from a manual.

The instruments weren't developed at a desk. They come from running our own sport-tourism operation — AirSwitch, a kite and wingfoil school at Le Morne that guests independently rank among the top-rated at its location, across 500+ public reviews on Tripadvisor and Google — the same discipline of guest scrutiny your own facility lives under, at any scale. The audit criteria are the same standards we hold our own operation to.

Where a project needs expertise outside sport science, such as turf agronomy or building services, we bring in qualified specialists rather than stretching our own claim. One rule governs every report: no claim without evidence. Scores are built from defined, weighted criteria; recommendations are ranked by impact and cost; and where something sits outside our competence, the report says so.
