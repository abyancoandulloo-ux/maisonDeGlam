✦ Our Story

The Art of Beauty

Where Mauritian elegance meets luxury makeup craftsmanship.

Crafted with Passion,
Worn with Confidence

Maison De Glam was born from a simple yet powerful belief: that makeup is not merely a product — it is an expression of identity, a celebration of individuality, and a daily ritual of self-love. Inspired by the vibrant energy and natural beauty of Mauritius, our atelier brings together the finest ingredients and the most exquisite pigments to create cosmetics that empower.

Every product in our collection is carefully curated and handcrafted to elevate your beauty routine. From the velvety texture of our lip oils to the luminous finish of our foundations, each piece is designed to make you feel as radiant as the island that inspires us.

We believe that luxury is not just about the product — it's about the experience. That's why we pour our heart into every detail, from the formulation to the packaging, ensuring that when you choose Maison De Glam, you're choosing excellence.

— Maison De Glam
